# Voiceover scripts

One page per clip, timed to the beats in `src/Ad.tsx`. Read it, save the file
as `public/audio/<id>-<lang>.mp3`, re-render. Nothing else to configure —
`npm run render:all` rebuilds the audio manifest first.

## Before you record

**Use your own voice.** ElevenLabs supports Central Kurdish for
speech-to-*text* but not text-to-speech, and the dedicated Kurdish TTS tools
that exist are of uncertain quality. A synthetic Kurdish voice that lands
slightly wrong costs you credibility with the exact native speakers you are
trying to reach — and they will say so in the comments. A phone voice memo in
a quiet room beats all of it.

**Leave the gaps.** The silence from 6.4s to 7.6s is the tension before the
answer. Do not fill it. Read the reveal line *after* the answer appears on
screen, not over the countdown.

**Stay under the clip.** Every script below fits 15 seconds at an unhurried
pace. If you are rushing, cut words rather than speeding up.

**Do not read the options aloud.** They are on screen. Reading them wastes
four seconds and gives people a reason to look away.

## Timing

| Say this | At | Screen shows |
|---|---|---|
| Hook line | 0.5s | stakes line appears |
| Question | 2.0s | options staggering in |
| *(silence)* | 3.4–7.6s | countdown, then the beat |
| Answer + reason | 7.8s | correct option turns green |
| Call to action | 12.6s | CTA appears |

       پێش ڕاگەیاندن سەیر بکە. ئاماژەدان لە سەرەتادا کەسێک هان دەدات کارێک بکات لەسەر جووڵەیەک کە هێشتا نەتپشکنیوە سەلامەتە.
