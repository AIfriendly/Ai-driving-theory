## pdf 037-041 (book 38-42) ch2 pedestrian types + passengers
- 38-39 PEDESTRIAN TYPES AT RISK. (1) CHILDREN: worst at judging distance/speed/direction; **short height means they cannot see over cars and drivers cannot see them**; self-control weakest, can switch behaviour in an instant; in residential areas slow down and **always expect a child to appear suddenly**. (2) TEENAGERS: risk-taking age; skateboards/scooters treated as pedestrians but faster and less predictable. (3) ELDERLY: hazard perception reduced, similar to children; cross slowly; **may stop suddenly in the middle of the road**; give way especially near care homes.
- 40 (4) INTOXICATED pedestrian - among the most dangerous; cannot control movement, may fall into the road or step in front of you. (5) SPECIAL NEEDS: **wheelchair users have a LOWER EYE LEVEL - they may not see you and you may not see them**; give extra room. **Deaf pedestrians: the horn is useless, and sounding it can frighten them.** Never block a pavement - if they cannot pass they must go into the road. **A mobility-device user on the pavement/crossing is treated as a PEDESTRIAN.**
- 41 (6) MENTAL ILLNESS: behaves like an intoxicated pedestrian; do not anger or stare, give way. (7) DISTRACTED PERSON on a phone - unaware, may step out suddenly. **Someone who dismounts a bike/scooter/motorcycle and walks it is treated as a pedestrian.** **BLIND: raising the cane means they intend to cross - you must stop and give priority. The blind person's cane is WHITE WITH A RED BAND.**
- 42 PASSENGERS / CHILD RESTRAINT. Driver is responsible for every passenger. **Child under 13 must use a child seat suited to weight and age, in the REAR.** A normal adult belt cannot protect a small child and **can cause worse injury in a crash**. **NEVER fit a child seat in the front where the airbag is active.** Weight bands: **up to 13 kg rear-facing; ~9-18 and 15-25 kg forward-facing in the rear; 22-36 kg BOOSTER SEAT.** **Engage the rear-door CHILD LOCK when carrying children or a person with mental illness.**
CANDIDATE GAPS: **white cane has a RED band; raising the cane = intent to cross; wheelchair lower eye level; deaf pedestrian + horn; child under 13 in the rear in a child seat; child seat never in front of a live airbag; booster seat 22-36 kg; child lock.**

## pdf 099-103 (book 100-104) ch5 gearbox / clutch / steering
- 100 **MANUAL GEARBOX PROS: more power; cheap and easy to repair; SAFER DOWNHILL AND ON SLIPPERY ROADS; less fuel if used correctly. CONS: harder to learn; too much pressure wears the CLUTCH out; tiring in congested traffic.** **AUTOMATIC: P Park, N Neutral, D Drive, R Reverse; selects gear by road speed, engine speed and manoeuvring need. - 102 **STEERING SYSTEM. POWER STEERING reduces the force needed. THREE TYPES: (1) HYDRAULIC - belt-driven off the engine, uses some engine power, the OLDEST type; (2) ELECTRO-HYDRAULIC - hydraulic pressure from an ELECTRIC MOTOR instead of an engine belt; (3) ELECTRIC (EPAS) - fully electronic, the LIGHTEST and smoothest.**
- 103 **STEERING WHEEL POSTURE: shoulders and back leaning slightly back; adjust wheel height with the adjuster underneath; set correctly you can see the dashboard and the front of the car; the lower rim must be below shoulder level with no pressure on the arms and a suitable bend in them.** **HOLD WITH TWO HANDS. RESTING A FOOT UP THERE traps the leg, thigh and pelvis and can injure the spine.**
