KNOWN SOURCE DEFECT (handled correctly, not an app error):
The exam gives the maximum reversing distance as **10 m in Q111** and **5 m in Q264**. The app
deliberately teaches the principle ("only a short distance, slowly, once the way behind is
clear") and its explanation states outright that the paper gives two different maxima. That is
the right call — it cannot be resolved from the source, and testing a contested number would
fail a learner unfairly.
