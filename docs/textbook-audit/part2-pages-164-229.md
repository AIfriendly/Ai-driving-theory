## b041-b047 (book 214-220)
- 214 t150-151: **first aid rests on 3 principles: preserve life, prevent worsening, speed recovery**. **Bleeding: EXTERNAL (from a wound) or FROM AN ORIFICE - nose, ear, mouth - which points to INTERNAL injury**
- 215 t151: arterial bright red and spurting vs venous dark red. Steps: **wear gloves**; calm them (fear and panic increase bleeding); expose the wound and cover with a clean cloth; **never pull out a large embedded object**; lay them down with minimum movement; **press on the wound**; call for help if it will not stop
- 216 t152: **internal bleeding signs (7): swelling and blue skin; raised heart rate; severe pain at the site; shivering and palpitations; rapid breathing; thirst; dizziness and unconsciousness.** Help: keep them calm; **lay them down and RAISE THE LEGS to chest level**; **nil by mouth**; call 122
- 217 t153-154: nosebleed - **sit, head FORWARD, pinch the soft cartilage 10-15 minutes**; **if it goes on more than 2 minutes get medical help**. Fracture closed vs open. **Fracture signs: pain and swelling; colour change often blue or purple; bending or deformity; inability to move the part**
- 218 t154: **cut the clothing away at the fracture site so you can see**; encourage minimum movement; **immobilise in place, splint with a board if possible**; cover a bleeding wound but **do not press on a protruding bone**; **a spinal fracture - any action can make it worse**; call 122; do not move unless in danger
- 219 t155: snake/scorpion signs - **two small puncture marks if venomous**, pain, swelling, colour change, blurred vision, headache and vomiting, muscle weakness, sweating and thirst. Do: sit or lay them safely away from the site; **call 122**; **remove bracelets, watches and rings from hands and feet**; **wash with soap and water**; cover with a clean cloth; **MARK THE BITE SITE AND THE LEVEL OF THE SWELLING**. Do NOT: chase or kill the animal; suck the venom; **tie or bind the site**; cut it; ice it; give coffee, alcohol or tea
- 220 t156: burns - remove from the source; **if electrical, make sure the power is OFF before approaching**; **take off rings, belts, metal objects and watches near the burn - it swells fast**; cover with something clean, no pressure; **water on the burn for at least 10-15 minutes**; call 122
